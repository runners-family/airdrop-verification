# Final eligibility criteria

The reference slot is **458,146,998**, selected October 17, 2026 at 19:08 UTC. Each category was collected fresh with its rule. Historical activity is bounded by the reference slot. Current balances and positions were read after it, using finalized responses at or above that slot; these are collection-window observations, not an exact historical account-state replay. Meteora current values were collected about 13 hours later.

## Cluster exclusions

The final lists apply the approved deterministic first-funder cluster screen. Labelled exchanges, issuers and services do not form exclusion clusters. An unlabelled cluster controlled by a person or team, without automated-hot-wallet behavior, is excluded when any of these holds:

- At least 25 participants, with at least 80% funded within 7 days and at least 80% receiving amounts within 2% of the median.
- At least 80% funded within 24 hours and at least 80% receiving near-identical amounts.
- At least half returning funds to the funder or a cluster-specific common destination, plus either the 7-day burst or near-identical amounts.

A listed funder operating an excluded cluster is also removed. The rule removed 3,386 participants across categories. These are screening decisions, not proof of personal identity. Only the resulting final category lists are included here; cluster dossiers and excluded-address lists are omitted.
